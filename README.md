# 3. Prompts Used

## Basic Prompt

```text id="1sdm52"
Create a realistic coffee-making video showing espresso preparation from scratch. Include grinding coffee beans, extracting espresso, frothing milk, pouring latte art, and serving the coffee in a cozy café environment with warm lighting and cinematic camera shots.
```

---

## Refined Prompt

```text id="6nkq90"
Create an ultra-realistic cinematic café video of premium handcrafted coffee prepared in a modern luxury coffee shop. Begin with aesthetic ingredient shots featuring roasted coffee beans, espresso machine tools, milk pitcher, and ceramic mugs arranged beautifully on a wooden counter. Show detailed close-up scenes of grinding coffee beans, espresso extraction with rich crema, steaming and frothing milk, and slow-motion latte art pouring into a glossy ceramic cup. Capture warm golden café lighting, cinematic steam effects, shallow depth of field, smooth camera transitions, realistic coffee textures, cozy ambiance, luxury coffee advertisement style, Instagram reels aesthetic, professional cinematography, and ultra-detailed 4K realism.
```

# Conclusion

By using detailed and well-crafted prompts, AI text-to-video generation models can effectively reproduce realistic coffee-making videos. The quality of the generated video depends on how accurately the prompt describes visual elements such as lighting, textures, camera movement, mood, and composition. The experiment demonstrated the importance of prompt refinement, cinematic detailing, and iterative improvement when generating realistic AI videos for creative and commercial applications.
